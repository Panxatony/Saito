## Bookmarks

A bookmark keeps a single posting so it can be found again later, however far
down the archive it has since travelled.

### Setting and removing

Every opened posting carries a bookmark icon. One click sets the bookmark,
another takes it back; a filled icon means the posting is kept. Bookmarks are
private — nobody else sees what you have kept.

### Finding them again

The bookmark icon in the header opens your collection, and each entry there
leads back to its posting.

Unlike the read markers, bookmarks do not lapse. They stay until you remove
them, or until the posting itself is deleted.
