## The widgets

Three small panels sit beside the thread list — to its right on wide screens,
below it on narrow ones.

- **Online** shows who is currently present. Signed-in members appear by name,
  guests and search engines only as a number. The word *users* in the heading
  leads to the full member list.
- **Recent entries** lists the forum's newest postings.
- **Your postings** lists your own, once you are signed in.

### Folding them away

A click on a heading shrinks a panel. On wide screens it shrinks to its icon at
the right edge; once all three are shrunk, the thread list takes the space that
frees up. On narrow screens only the content folds away, because icons at the
edge would gain nothing there.

Where a heading contains a link — *users* in the online panel, for instance — a
click follows the link rather than closing the panel.

### Where the arrangement is kept

Signed in, the arrangement is stored on your account and therefore applies on
every device. Without an account the browser remembers it, and only there.

When shrunk, the online icon carries the number of signed-in members. Guests and
search engines are not counted: the number is meant to say how many people are
there to talk to.
