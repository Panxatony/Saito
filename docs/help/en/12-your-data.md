## Your data

Everything this forum holds about you can be downloaded as a file — in your own
settings, right next to *change password*.

That is not a courtesy but a right: Articles 15 and 20 of the GDPR entitle every
person to a copy of their data, in a form a machine can read. So the file is
JSON, but formatted to be read by a person as well — open it in any text editor
and go through it.

### What is in it

The account, with name, email address, join date and signature. Every display
preference. **All of your own postings in full**, with date, category and
subject. Drafts not yet posted. Your uploads with their names and addresses —
not the files themselves, which live at those addresses already and would swell
the download several times over. Bookmarks with their notes. Blocks against your
account, with the reason given. Your own ignore list.

### What is deliberately absent

**Your password.** It exists only as a value that cannot be turned back into the
password, and a file people download, mail to themselves and forget in a
Downloads folder is the last place for it.

**Other people.** Who ignores *you* is those people's decision and not your
disclosure. Which moderator imposed a block is not in it — the reason is, since
that concerns you. And other people's postings are absent even where you edited
them as a moderator: the record says that you did, with an id and a date, but
carries none of their text.

The GDPR says so in as many words: the copy must not come at the expense of
other people's rights.

### What for

To see what is actually stored. To move to another forum. Or simply to have
twenty years of your own writing in one place, in case the forum one day is not
there.

The button only ever hands out your own data — not even an administrator can
trigger it for somebody else.
