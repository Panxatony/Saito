## RSS feeds

The forum offers its postings as RSS, so a feed reader can collect them without
anyone opening the site.

### Public feeds

Two feeds are open to everyone and are linked in the page footer:

- **New postings** — every new posting, answers included
- **New threads** — only the opening postings

They carry only what is readable without signing in. Browsers and feed readers
also find them by themselves, because every page points at them.

### Personal feeds

Your profile offers the same two feeds again, this time with a personal token in
the address. That version additionally carries the areas visible only when
signed in.

The token stands in for signing in, so **treat these addresses like a
password**: anyone holding one reads along without an account. It is derived
from your password, which means **changing your password invalidates the old
feed addresses** — they have to be replaced in the reader with the new ones.
That is also how an address handed out by accident is taken out of service
again.
